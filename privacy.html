Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what the Velachery Dreamers club app ("the app") collects, why, who can see it, and how to remove it. The app is run by the Velachery Dreamers running club, Chennai, for its members. All members are adults (18+).

What we collect

  • From Google sign-in: your name, email address and profile photo. You can change the name shown in the club on the Me tab.
  • From Strava, only if you choose to connect it, and only your runs (Run, Trail Run, Virtual Run) from 1 January 2026 onwards:
    • start date and time, distance, moving and elapsed time, and elevation gain;
    • a simplified route line of the run.
    We do not store heart rate, calories or any other health data, activity titles, photos, or any other kind of activity (rides, walks, swims…).
  • Shown to you only, never stored: when you open one of your runs, or add them to your own run card, the app asks Strava for that run's per-km splits and average / maximum heart rate (if your watch recorded it). These are shown on your screen or on the image you choose to save, and are not kept by the club.
  • Club activity: coach-session check-ins (attendance), event and team membership, your role (member, coach or admin), the daily, weekly and monthly distance goals you set, your accent colour, and whether you appear on the yearly leaderboard.

The app does not use your phone's location. Session check-in works by scanning the coach's QR code.

Why we use it

Only to run the club: your own stats, streak, goals and charts; the yearly club leaderboard; team events and their results; coach-session attendance; and summary images you can choose to download and share.

Who can see what

  • Only you can see your individual runs, daily, weekly and monthly stats, pace, streak, goals and route lines.
  • Yearly Run leaderboard: every club member can see your name, photo, total distance and number of runs for the year. You can hide yourself at any time (Me → "Show me on the leaderboard").
  • Team events: your teammates can see your distance in that event. Coaches and admins see each team's total. Final results (team names and total distances) are posted in the club's in-app News.
  • Attendance: coaches and admins see who attended each session, and may post monthly or per-session attendance tables in the club's in-app News.
  • Club members can see your name, photo and role.
  • Your email address is visible only to you and the club admins.

Images you share

You can save images of your own daily, weekly and monthly stats, and of a single run, to your phone and share them wherever you like. Run images show your route on a street map with the first and last 200 m removed, so they don't reveal where you start or finish. Club results and attendance tables can't be saved from the app; they are only posted in the club's in-app News.

What we don't do

We do not sell your data, show ads, use your data to train AI models, or share it with anyone outside the club, except with the service providers below, who process it only to run the app.

Service providers

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and the server code that talks to Strava.
  • Google: sign-in.
  • Strava: source of run data, used according to Strava's API Agreement. You can revoke access at any time in the app (Me → Strava → Disconnect) or on strava.com under Settings → My Apps.
  • OpenStreetMap: street-map images for your run cards. When a run card is shown, your phone downloads map images of that area from OpenStreetMap's servers, which receive your IP address and the map area requested. No account or personal details are sent.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Strava at any time, in the app or on strava.com. We stop receiving runs and delete every run we imported from Strava, including routes.
  • When you edit or delete a run on Strava, the change reaches the app automatically.
  • Hide yourself from the yearly leaderboard on the Me tab.
  • Delete your account and data: email velacherydreamers@gmail.com from your registered email. We delete your profile, runs, goals, attendance and Strava connection within 30 days.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Database access rules enforce the visibility above. Strava access is handled only by the app's server: your Strava login tokens are never sent to your phone or to other members.

Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the date at the top. Significant changes are also announced in the app's News.
