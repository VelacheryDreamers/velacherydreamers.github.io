Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy explains what the Velachery Dreamers club app ("the app") collects, why, who can see it, and how to remove it. The app is run by the Velachery Dreamers running club, Chennai, for its members. All members are adults (18+).

What we collect

  • From Google sign-in: your name, email address and profile photo.
  • From Strava, only if you choose to connect it: your activities, meaning type, date and time, distance, duration, pace, elevation, heart rate (if recorded) and route map.
  • Club activity: coach-session check-ins (attendance), event and team membership, and your role (member, coach or admin).
  • Location at check-in: if a session uses location confirmation, your phone's location is checked once when you scan the session QR code. It is not tracked otherwise.

Why we use it

Only to run the club: show your own stats and charts, weekly and monthly leaderboards, streaks, coach-session attendance, team events and winners, and summary images you can choose to download and share.

Who can see what

  • Club members can see your name, photo, role, and club totals such as distance, run counts, streaks, attendance and event results.
  • Your route maps and detailed activity data are visible only to you.
  • Your email address is visible only to you and the club admins.
  • Coaches can see attendance for the sessions they run.

What we don't do

We do not sell your data, show ads or share your data with anyone outside the club, except with the service providers below, who process it only to run the app.

Service providers

  • Supabase: database and sign-in. Data is stored in the Mumbai (India) region.
  • Google: sign-in.
  • Strava: source of activity data, used according to Strava's API Agreement. You can revoke access at any time in Strava under Settings → My Apps.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Strava at any time in the app, or in Strava's settings. We stop receiving new activities.
  • Delete your account and data: email velacherydreamers@gmail.com from your registered email. We delete your profile, activities, attendance and Strava connection within 30 days.
  • When you delete an activity on Strava, it is also removed from the app.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Database access rules enforce the visibility above. Strava access tokens are stored server-side and are never visible to other members.

Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the date at the top.
