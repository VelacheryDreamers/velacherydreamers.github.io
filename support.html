Support

Help for members using the Velachery Dreamers club app.

Need help? Email velacherydreamers@gmail.com from the email you sign in with. Tell us your name, what you were doing and what you saw (a screenshot helps). We usually reply within 2 days.

Connecting Strava

  • In the app, open Me → Strava → Connect with Strava, read the consent screen, then tap Allow on Strava's page.
  • Leave "View data about your activities" ticked, or your runs can't be counted.
  • If Strava says the athlete limit has been reached, the club's Strava connection is full for now. Email us and we'll let you know when a place opens.
  • One Strava account can be connected to only one club member.

My runs aren't showing

  • Check the run is on Strava first. Your watch app (Garmin Connect, Coros, Amazfit…) must upload to Strava.
  • On the app's Home screen, pull down to refresh, or tap Me → Strava → Sync now.
  • Only runs count (Run, Trail Run, Virtual Run). Rides, walks and other activities are not imported.
  • If the Strava card says "Reconnect needed", tap Connect again.
  • If the same run arrived twice, the app asks you on Home to confirm whether it should count.

Leaderboard and privacy

  • Only you can see your individual runs, routes, splits and heart rate.
  • To leave the Yearly Run leaderboard, turn off Me → Show me on the leaderboard.
  • Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Disconnect or delete

  • Disconnect Strava: Me → Strava → Disconnect, or on strava.com under Settings → My Apps. Your imported runs are deleted from the club app.
  • Delete your account: email velacherydreamers@gmail.com from your registered email. We delete your data within 30 days.

Installing or updating the app

  • The app is shared by the club as an Android install file (APK). If your phone asks, allow "Install unknown apps" for the app you opened it from.
  • To update, install the new file over the old one. Your account and data stay.
