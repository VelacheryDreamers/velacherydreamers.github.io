A running club in Velachery, Chennai. Our members' app brings the club together in one place:

  • Weekly and monthly leaderboards and run streaks, from Strava
  • Coach sessions with QR check-in
  • Team challenges, events and winners
  • Shareable run summaries for your stories

The app is available to club members only. To join, contact velacherydreamers@gmail.com.
